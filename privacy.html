Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

HillsBay is a local marketplace for Nagaland where people buy and sell items and services. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. HillsBay is operated by HillsBay ("we", "us").

Information you give us

  • Email address and password — used to create your account and sign you in. We send one-time codes to your email to confirm it and to reset your password. Your email is never shown to other users. Passwords are stored only in hashed (scrambled) form.
  • Contact number (optional) — if you add one, it is shown only to signed-in buyers and only if you turn on "Show my number to buyers" in Settings.
  • Profile — your name, optional photo, town and district, and an optional short bio. These are shown publicly on your profile and listings.
  • Google account details — only if you choose "Continue with Google": your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Listings — titles, descriptions, prices, category details, town, the optional area or village name you add, and photos you post. Live listings are public.
  • Messages and offers — chat messages, photos sent in chat and offers you make or receive. These are visible only to the two people in the conversation, except as described under "Safety and moderation".
  • Reports and blocks — reports you file and users you block.
  • Shops — if you open a shop: the shop name, logo, cover and gallery photos, description, address, phone and WhatsApp numbers, opening hours, delivery and pickup details and the other shop page details you add. These are shown publicly on your shop page.
  • Enquiries and reviews — when you send a shop an enquiry (for example a custom cake, table or viewing request) we send that shop your message, the details you fill in and the phone or WhatsApp number you give for the reply. Shop reviews and ratings you write are public.
  • Saved shops — shops you save with the heart, visible only to you.
  • Purchases — if you buy a paid feature (such as a boost or a shop plan), a record of the order: what you bought, the price, the time and the Google Play order reference.

Information collected automatically

  • Activity needed to run the service — for example favourites, saved searches, listing view counts (counted once per signed-in user per day) and when you were last active, which may be shown to people you chat with (for example "Active 5m ago").
  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, a device token so we can alert you about messages and offers.
  • Technical data — like any online service, our hosting provider may log IP addresses and request details for security and reliability.
  • Crash reports — if the app hits an error, it sends us the error message, the screen you were on, your phone model, Android version and app version (and your account ID if you are signed in), so we can fix it. Kept for 30 days.

Your recently viewed listings and recent searches are stored only on your device.

Location

If you tap "Use my current location", HillsBay reads your location once, on your device, to pick the nearest town and suggest the name of your area or village using your phone's built-in map service. Your coordinates are not sent to our servers or shown to anyone. We store only the town and, if you keep it, the area name — you can edit or remove it before posting. Distances between listings are calculated from public town-centre coordinates, never from anyone's exact location.

Voice search (optional)

The microphone button in the search box opens your phone's own voice typing (for example Google). HillsBay does not record audio and never gets the microphone permission; it only receives the words you said, as search text.

Sell with AI (optional)

If you choose to use "Sell with AI", the photos you select and any note you add are sent to Google's Gemini AI service, which suggests a title, description, category, condition and price range for your listing. We keep the suggested text so we don't have to ask again, and we record how many AI requests you make so we can apply monthly limits. AI suggestions can be wrong: you review and edit everything, and nothing is published until you publish it yourself. You never have to use AI to sell on HillsBay.

Ads

HillsBay shows two kinds of ads, both clearly labelled.

  • Local ads — ads from local businesses between listings, marked "Ad". We count how many times each is shown and tapped in total, but we don't tell advertisers who saw or tapped their ad and don't share your personal information with them. Tapping an ad's button opens the advertiser's phone number, WhatsApp or website, which is outside HillsBay.
  • Google ads — a banner marked "Advertisement" served by Google AdMob. To show and measure these ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), and may show personalised ads where the law allows. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent message appears first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Payments

Paid features in HillsBay (boosts, featured listings, extra listings and shop plans) are bought through Google Play's billing system. Google handles the payment: we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. RevenueCat, our purchase-verification provider, receives the purchase details from Google Play so our server can confirm a purchase is real before the feature is switched on. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in the Google Play Store.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your listings and profile to buyers and let people chat and make offers.
  • To send notifications you have enabled (messages, offers, enquiries, listing updates).
  • To confirm purchases and switch on the paid features you bought.
  • To show ads (see "Ads").
  • To keep the marketplace safe: detecting spam and prohibited items, enforcing limits, reviewing reports, and acting against misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the Google ads described above, we do not share it with advertisers.

Who can see your information

  • Everyone: live listings (with photos, price and town) and your public profile (name, photo, town, bio, member-since date, verification badge).
  • People you chat with: your messages, chat photos, offers and last-active time.
  • A shop you send an enquiry to: your name, the enquiry details and the reply number you give.
  • Signed-in buyers: your contact number, only if you add one and turn on "Show my number to buyers".
  • Our moderators: listings, reports, and any chat message that has been reported, so they can investigate.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — database, file storage, sign-in and real-time chat.
  • Expo (650 Industries) — app builds and app updates.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Email delivery provider — sending account confirmation and password-reset codes to your email.
  • Google Play — app distribution and payments for paid features; RevenueCat — verifying those purchases.
  • Google AdMob — the Google ads described above.
  • Google — only if you use Google sign-in, and Google Gemini — only for photos and notes you send with Sell with AI.

These providers may store data on servers outside India.

Safety and moderation

To protect users, we automatically check listings and messages for prohibited words and suspicious links, apply posting and messaging limits, and may review content that is reported. We may remove content and warn, suspend or ban accounts that break our Terms. We may share information with law enforcement where required by law or to prevent fraud or harm.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. Expired and sold listings stay in your account until you delete them.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, listings, favourites, chats, offers and notifications. Some records may remain for a short time in backups, or longer where we must keep them by law (for example to deal with fraud).

No longer have the app? Email hillsbay39@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and listings at any time.
  • Control notifications and phone-number visibility in Settings.
  • Block users so they cannot message you.
  • Delete your account in the app.
  • Ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, or raise a concern, by emailing hillsbay39@gmail.com.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by database security rules so that, for example, only conversation members can read a chat. No system is perfectly secure, so please never share one-time codes, UPI PINs or passwords with anyone.

Children

HillsBay is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
