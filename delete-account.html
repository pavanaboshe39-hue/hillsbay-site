Delete your HillsBay account

Last updated 16 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  • Open HillsBay and sign in.
  • Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email hillsbay39@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will check the request comes from you and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo and contact number.
  • Your listings and their photos, your shop and its photos, offers and enquiries.
  • Your chats, favourites, saved shops, saved searches and notifications.

What may be kept

  • Records of paid purchases, kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Reports about your account and safety records, where needed to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Copies in backups for a short time, until they are overwritten.

Subscriptions bought through Google Play are not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel them in the Google Play Store first.
